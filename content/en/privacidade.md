## In short

- **What you type does not leave your device.** Teclado360 works without internet and sends no text, words or usage
  statistics to anyone, including us.
- **No ads, no tracking, no usage analytics**, and no third-party libraries inside the app.
- **The keyboard learns words only on your device**, to suggest better. You can erase everything it has learned
  whenever you want.
- **In password fields and private fields**, the keyboard does not suggest, learn or store anything.

## 2. What stays on your device

To work, the keyboard stores on the device itself, in the app's private storage:

| What | What for | How to erase it |
|---|---|---|
| Words you used more than once that are not in the dictionary | To suggest them in the suggestion row and when swiping | "Forget learned words" in the keyboard settings, or uninstalling the app |
| The symbols you used last in formula mode | To show them first | Same option as above |
| Your preferences (language, suggestions, learning, floating keyboard) | To keep the keyboard the way you left it | Restoring the defaults in the settings, or uninstalling |

This data is not included in the system's automatic backup and is not sent to us. You can turn learning off in the
settings; with it off, the keyboard does not store new words.

## 3. What the keyboard never stores

- The text you type. The keyboard only looks at a few characters around the cursor, at the moment you type, to add
  accents, capitalize and suggest the next word. None of it is saved.
- Anything from password fields or fields the app marked as private. In them, the keyboard does not suggest, learn or
  swipe.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not collect personal data through the keyboard and have no server that receives what you type.
- We do not sell, rent or share data.
- We show no ads and build no usage profiles, of adults or of children and adolescents.

The notice Android shows when you turn on any keyboard ("this input method may be able to collect all the text you
type") is standard and appears for every keyboard. Teclado360 does not ask for internet access. On iOS, the keyboard
works without "Full Access".

## 5. Data handled by the stores and the system

Some information is handled by Google or Apple, under their own privacy policies, not by us:

- **Purchase and payment:** done by the store. We receive from the store only what it passes on to every developer,
  such as the order number, to handle refunds and questions.
- **Crash reports:** if you allowed it on your device, the system sends Google or Apple technical crash reports
  (device model, system version, the point in the program where the crash happened). We see these reports in
  aggregate, to fix defects. They do not contain what you type.

## 6. Children and adolescents

Teclado360 is used by students. Since the app collects no personal data, it already ships with the most protective
settings possible (Brazilian Law No. 15,211/2025, art. 7), builds no profiles and shows no advertising (art. 22).
Purchases and downloads by minors follow the stores' parental supervision rules.

## 7. Your rights

The LGPD (art. 18) lets you confirm whether we process your data, access, correct and delete it, ask for
portability, know with whom it was shared and revoke consent. Since the keyboard's data stays only on your device,
you can see and erase it yourself (item 2). For any request, use the channel in item 1; we reply within 15 days.

You may also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd.

## 8. Security

The data in item 2 stays in the app's private storage, which the system protects from other apps. If a security
incident may bring relevant risk or harm, we will notify you and the ANPD (LGPD, art. 48).

## 9. This website

This website uses no cookies, has no visit analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Your choice between the
light and dark theme is stored only in your browser. GitHub, which hosts the site, may log technical access data,
such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with the new date, and the app tells you the first time
it is opened after the change.
