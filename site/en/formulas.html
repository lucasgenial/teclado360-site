Formula mode

The math keyboard that shows up in formula fields, with the gallery of structures and the formula drawing.

In fields the app marked for formulas, Teclado360 swaps the text keyboard for the math keyboard. You build the formula and it appears drawn as in a textbook while you type. In ordinary fields, nothing changes: the text keyboard gets no new keys.

The screens

Screen What it has
123 Numbers, x and n, fractions, powers (□², □ⁿ), roots, parentheses, absolute value, π, e, ∞, ≤ and ≥
f(x) sen, cos, tg, log, ln, limit, Σ, ∫, derivative, quantifiers, sets, arrows, vector and angle
abc Letters, for variables and names
αβγ Greek letters, lowercase and uppercase
□⁄□… The gallery of structures

The last row is the same on every screen: ← and → move the cursor, ⇥ goes to the next blank and ↵ delivers the formula. Keys with a dot in the corner have more options: hold to see them (on =, for example, ≠, ≈, ≡, ≅ and ∼).

The function names follow Brazilian notation (sen, tg); in English, sin and tan are on the same keys, by holding them.

Gallery of structures

The □⁄□… key opens the gallery, organized like Word's equation ribbon: fraction, script, radical, integral, large operator, delimiters, function, accent, limit and log, operator and matrix. Each structure appears drawn, the way it will look. Tap one and it goes into the formula with blanks (□) for you to fill in.

Blanks and what you already typed

  • A structure takes along what is to the left of the cursor when it makes sense: typing 12 and tapping □/□ puts 12 in the numerator and the cursor in the denominator.
  • ⇥ moves to the next blank. Typing in a selected blank fills it.
  • ⌫ deletes to the left; at the start of a structure, it takes the structure apart and keeps what was inside.

Typed shortcuts

Type the word and it becomes the symbol:

Type Becomes
sqrt or raiz √
pi π
alpha, beta, theta, delta… α, β, θ, δ…
infty ∞
sin, cos, tan, log, ln the functions
<=, >=, !=, +-, -> ≤, ≥, ≠, ±, →

A shortcut that can still grow waits for the word to end: cos only becomes cosine when you type something else, because it could still become cossec.

Tap on the formula

Tap anywhere on the drawn formula to put the cursor there. On the top half of a fraction, the cursor goes to the numerator; on a matrix cell, to the cell. Tapping a parenthesis puts the cursor outside it, and tapping a blank selects it.

Matrices and systems

With the cursor in a matrix or a system, you can insert a row above or below, a column to the left or right, and delete the row or the column, as in Word. New cells come with a blank. The last row and column stay; to delete the whole matrix, use ⌫. A system has at most 2 columns, and matrices, 10.

What goes into the field

The app chooses the format when it marks the field:

  • Readable text (Unicode): x²+1, ½, √2. It works anywhere.
  • LaTeX: x^2+1, \frac{1}{2}, \sqrt{2}. It is the format of Gabarito360 and Editor360.

Read aloud

Every formula has a reading for the screen reader, in Brazilian Portuguese: x² + 1 is read "x ao quadrado mais 1" ("x squared plus 1").
