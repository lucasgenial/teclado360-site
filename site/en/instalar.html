Install and turn on

How to install Teclado360, turn it on and switch keyboards whenever you want.

Teclado360 is a keyboard like the others on your device: once installed, it shows up in any app you type in.

Android

  1. Install Teclado360 from Google Play.
  2. Open the app and tap Turn on Teclado360. It takes you to the system screen where keyboards are turned on.
  3. Turn on Teclado360. Android shows a standard notice, which appears for any keyboard; Teclado360 does not ask for internet access, and what you type does not leave your device (learn more).
  4. Go back to the app and tap Choose Teclado360.

The path to the system screen varies a little by manufacturer. It is usually Settings → System → Keyboard → On-screen keyboard. On Samsung Galaxy devices, it is Settings → General management → Keyboard list and default.

iPhone and iPad

The iOS app comes after version 1.0. When it does, you will turn it on in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard. Teclado360 works without "Full Access".

In Gabarito360 and Editor360

In the products of the Gabarito360 ecosystem, Teclado360 comes with the page itself, with nothing to install. On the first field, you are asked "Which keyboard should I use here?": tap Teclado360 or Device keyboard. You can switch whenever you want in your profile.

Switch keyboards

  • Right away: on screen 3/3, the globe key switches to the next keyboard on your device.
  • For good: choose another keyboard in the system settings. Teclado360 stays installed and comes back when you choose it again.

Uninstall

Uninstall it like any app. The words the keyboard learned and your preferences go with it, because they only live on the device.
