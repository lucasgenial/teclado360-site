For text, the keyboard you know, smarter.

Letters in the usual order, with math and science symbols close at hand. Nothing changes on its own: the text only changes when you tap.

Screens of the Teclado360 web version, in Portuguese. The Android app follows the same design.

Formulas drawn as in a textbook.

In fields marked for formulas, Teclado360 becomes a math keyboard. Every structure in the gallery below was drawn by the keyboard itself.

Gallery of structures

Fractions, roots, integrals, sums, matrices and systems, organized like Word's equation ribbon.

Tap on the formula

Tap anywhere on the drawing to put the cursor there: in the numerator, in a cell, after a parenthesis.

LaTeX or readable text

The field receives LaTeX, for Gabarito360 and Editor360, or text like x²+1 that works anywhere.

Questions

Does it need internet?

No. Teclado360 runs entirely on the device, including suggestions and formula drawing.

How much does it cost?

The app is a one-time purchase in the store. The price appears on the app's page at launch, and updates cost nothing extra.

What if I change my mind?

You can withdraw within 7 days and get your money back, through the store or our support. See the license agreement.

Does it work in any app?

Yes, like any keyboard. Formula mode shows up only in fields the app marks for formulas; elsewhere you get the text keyboard with symbols at hand.

Can my app offer formula mode?

Yes. Just mark the field, as the page for app developers explains.
