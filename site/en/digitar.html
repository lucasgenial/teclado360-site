Typing

Accents, symbols, swiping, the suggestion row and what changes in each kind of field.

Keyboard screens

The text keyboard has the letters and three screens of numbers and symbols. The dot in a key's corner means it has more options: hold it to see them.

Screen How to get there What it has
Letters ABC Letters in the usual order (QWERTY), ⇧, ⌫, space and ↵
1/3 ?123 Numbers and operations
2/3 1/3 → 2/3 ², √, π, ≤, ≥ and arrows
3/3 2/3 → 3/3 Greek letters, sets, standalone accents, ç and the globe key

Capitals: one tap on ⇧ capitalizes the next letter; two taps lock it. Capitals also come on their own at the start of the text and after . ? and !.

Accents and symbols at hand

Hold the letter and pick the accent in the bubble. The bubble stays open after you let go: one tap picks, tapping outside closes it.

  • a → á à â ã ä ª
  • e → é ê è
  • o → ó ô õ º
  • c → ç

Swipe to type

Slide your finger across the letters without lifting it. The word goes into the field, capitalized at the start of a sentence and with a space before it when needed. A trail shows the path.

Right after, up to three other words show up in the row above: tapping one swaps the word. ⌫ right after deletes the whole word.

Swiping works on the letters keyboard. It does not work in passwords, e-mail, web addresses, private fields or the symbol screens.

Suggestion row

A fixed row above the letters, with three slots that never change places:

  • in the middle, the best suggestion;
  • on the left, the second best;
  • on the right, what you typed, in quotes.

It fixes accents (voce → você), completes the word (prof → professor) and catches a neighboring key (prifessor → professor). Nothing changes on its own: the text only changes when you tap. With no word typed, the row shows symbols: the ones you used last and π √ ≤ ≥ ° ² ±.

The keyboard learns your words

A word that is not in the dictionary starts being suggested after you use it twice, or right away if you tap it in quotes in the suggestion row. What the keyboard learns stays only on your device and never comes from a private field. To erase it all, use Forget learned words in the keyboard settings.

The keyboard follows the field

  • Numbers: a number field gets the numeric keyboard, with a comma (or a dot, in English). Phone fields get the +.
  • E-mail and web addresses: the letters screen brings @, the dot and the keys these fields use.
  • ↵: changes with the field: Next goes to the next field, Done closes, Search searches and, in long text, New line.
  • Private fields: in passwords, PINs, one-time codes and card numbers, there is no suggestion row or swiping, and the keyboard learns no words.

Floating and docked

On tablets and with the phone sideways, the keyboard can float. The ⧉ key switches between docked at the bottom and floating; to move the floating keyboard, drag the handle above it. The device remembers the mode and the place.

Accessibility

Every key has a name for the screen reader, such as "Symbols, page 1 of 3", "A acute" and "Capital for the next letter". With TalkBack, you explore the keyboard by touch and type the key with a double tap. In password fields, the reader says "Dot" instead of the letter.
